TripFam Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data we process when you use the TripFam app (iOS and Android) and our invite page at travelapp-31255.web.app, why we do it, who receives data and what rights you have. If the English and German versions differ, the German version prevails.

1. Controller

We are not required to appoint a data protection officer. For privacy questions, write to the email address above.

2. Summary

  • We only process data the app's features need. We do not sell data, show ads or use tracking or analytics tools for marketing.
  • Your data is stored with Google Firebase. The database is located in Frankfurt am Main, Germany.
  • We use your location only with your permission and never store exact coordinates.
  • You can delete your account and its data yourself in the app at any time.

3. What we process and why

3.1 Using the app as a guest

You can try TripFam without an account. The app then creates an anonymous account with Firebase Authentication that consists only of a random user ID, so we can keep your favourites and swipes. If you create an account later, this data moves with you.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app).

3.2 Your account

For an account we process your email address, your password (Firebase Authentication stores it only as a hash; we never see it), your display name and, optionally, a profile picture and your PayPal.Me name. Members of your trips see your display name, profile picture and PayPal.Me name. Nobody but you sees your email address. To reset your password, Firebase sends you an email.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.3 Trips and groups

When you create or join a trip, we store what you create together and make it available to the trip's members:

  • the trip's title, city, dates, members and invite code,
  • your swipes and votes on places, the wishlist and the day plans,
  • expenses with amount, payer and participants; for scanned receipts also the line items and who takes which item,
  • shared packing items and your packing progress as a percentage (your own packing list stays private),
  • journal entries with text and up to six photos each.

People invited by link or code can open the invite page in a browser. It shows only the trip's title, city and the inviting person's name.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.4 Discovering places, favourites and reviews

We store which places you saved and which you have already seen in the swipe deck, so you don't get them twice. If you review a place, the stars, text and your display name are visible to other signed-in users. If you report a review, we store the report. Hidden-gem tips you send us for your city are seen only by you and our team.

The place data itself (names, descriptions, opening hours, photos) comes from OpenStreetMap, Wikidata, Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and Overture Maps and contains no data about you.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.5 Location

With your permission, the app uses your location while it is open, for two things:

  • Places nearby: the app works out on your device which map areas (cells of about 5 km) are around you and loads the places in those areas. Your exact coordinates are not sent to us or stored.
  • Stamp book check-in: when you are at a place, you can check in. We then store only the city and the time, no coordinates.

Without permission, the app shows places in a city you choose. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your device settings.

Legal basis: consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act).

3.6 Scanning receipts

When you take or choose a photo of a receipt, the app sends it through Firebase AI Logic to Google's Gemini model. Gemini reads the items and amounts, and we store only that result in the expense. We do not store the photo itself. Google processes the photo as our processor and, under the terms of the paid Gemini API, does not use it to improve its own products. To prevent abuse, scans are limited per day. Please photograph only the receipt, not other people or documents.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you actively use the feature).

3.7 Camera, photos and calendar

The app accesses the camera and photo library only when you add a profile picture, journal photo or receipt yourself. It accesses the calendar only when you add a trip to your calendar; the events are then created directly on your device.

Legal basis: consent through your device's permission prompt, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG.

3.8 Push notifications and reminders

If you allow notifications, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token together with your user ID and app language. We use it to tell you when someone joins your trip, a place becomes a match, or an expense or journal entry is added. Delivery runs through Apple (APNs) or Google. Reminders, such as the evening before a trip, are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn notifications off in the app settings or your device settings.

Legal basis: consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG.

3.9 Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a report to Google through Firebase Crashlytics. It contains technical details such as the stack trace, device model, operating system and app version and a random installation ID, but no content from your trips. Google keeps these reports for 90 days. We use them only to find and fix errors.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a stable app) and § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

3.10 Abuse protection

With Firebase App Check we verify that requests to our services come from a genuine TripFam app. The app uses your operating system's attestation services for this (Apple DeviceCheck or Google Play Integrity). Only a confirmation that the app and device are genuine is exchanged, no content.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (protecting our services and costs from abuse) and § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

3.11 Maps

We show maps using Mapbox. When a map loads, technical data such as your IP address and device and app information is sent to Mapbox. According to Mapbox, its SDK also sends de-identified location and usage data to Mapbox to improve its maps (Mapbox telemetry).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for showing maps, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for telemetry.

3.12 Place photos and content

Place photos are delivered through Cloudflare's R2 storage service. When they load, Cloudflare necessarily receives your IP address and device details. The app keeps photos and place data in a cache on your device so they load faster and work offline.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

3.13 Links to other services

From the app you can open other services, such as Apple Maps or Google Maps for directions, a place's website, Wikipedia or a PayPal.Me link. This only happens when you tap the link. From then on, that provider's privacy policy applies.

3.14 Invite page in the browser

Our invite page is served through Firebase Hosting. When it is opened, Google processes technically necessary data such as IP address, time and browser details in server logs. The page sets no tracking cookies.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (delivering the page securely and reliably).

3.15 Storage on your device

The app stores settings such as language, appearance and push preference, plus a cache of your trip data, locally on your device so it also works without internet. This data leaves your device only through the features described above.

Legal basis: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4. Recipients and processors

We work with the following service providers. They process data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR):

  • Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, with Google LLC (USA): Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (located in Frankfurt am Main), Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions (Frankfurt am Main), Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, App Check, Hosting and Firebase AI Logic with Gemini.
  • Mapbox Inc., USA: maps.
  • Cloudflare, Inc., USA: storing and delivering place photos.

For delivering push notifications and verifying the app, the app also uses the services of Apple or Google as operating system providers.

Within the app, other users see only what section 3 describes, mainly the members of your trips. We do not pass data to any other third parties unless we are legally required to.

5. Transfers to third countries

Google, Mapbox and Cloudflare may also process data in the USA. All three are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has issued an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). The European Commission's standard contractual clauses also apply (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

6. How long we keep data

  • We keep account and trip data as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, the app deletes your profile, swipes, favourites, reviews, hidden-gem tips, packing lists, profile and journal photos, and trips where you are the only member. You are removed from shared trips. Expenses and ideas the other members need for their balances and plans stay there without your name. Google also deletes your sign-in data from backups within 180 days.
  • Google deletes crash reports after 90 days.
  • We delete push tokens when you turn notifications off, sign out or delete your account.

7. Your rights

You have the right to

  • access your data (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR); you can delete your account yourself at any time under Settings → Delete account,
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR),
  • withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR), for example through the permissions in your device settings.

Just write to the email address in section 1. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live or the one responsible for us, the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

8. Providing data and automated decisions

You are not obliged to provide data, but some features cannot be used without it, for example shared trips without an account. We do not make automated decisions or use profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

9. Minimum age

TripFam is intended for people aged 18 and over.

10. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
