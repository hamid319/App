# SwipeTrip — Logo Designer Brief

## Brand name

**SwipeTrip**

Recommended styling: **SwipeTrip** (title case, one word).

**Name breakdown:** *Swipe* (the core gesture—quick left/right decisions on place cards) + *Trip* (travel, journeys, destinations). The name should feel active and social, not static or corporate.

---

## One-liner

Discover travel spots by swiping—alone or with friends—until you match on where to go.

---

## What the app does

SwipeTrip is a **mobile travel-discovery app** that helps people decide *where* to go and *what* to see. Instead of endless lists and reviews, users browse places through a **card-swipe interface** familiar from dating apps—but applied to restaurants, sights, and local spots.

**Solo mode:** Users swipe through places near their current location (GPS-based). Swiping right saves a place to a personal favorites list; swiping left skips it. They can open place details, see photos and ratings, and launch directions in maps.

**Group mode:** Friends create or join a group, pick a destination (country and city), and run a **group swipe session**. Everyone swipes through the same pool of places. When enough group members like the same place, it surfaces as a **Group Match**—a shared pick the whole group can agree on.

SwipeTrip is about **decision-making made fun**, not booking flights, building itineraries, or luxury travel branding. It sits between “I don’t know what to do” and “we all actually want to go there.”

## Target audience

- **Friends and couples** planning weekends, city breaks, or group trips.
- **Young adults** who are comfortable with swipe-based mobile UX.
- **Mobile-first** users; the product is a native app (iOS/Android), not a web dashboard.
- **International audience** — the app is built for broad use; the logo should not rely on language-specific visuals.

---

## Core features (context for the designer)

- Swipe to like or pass on places
- Personal favorites and wishlist
- Place detail views with photos, ratings, and map links
- Travel groups with invite codes
- Destination selection (country and city)
- Synchronized group swipe sessions
- **Group Matches** — places the group collectively liked

## Visual direction hints

These are **starting points**, not strict rules. Creative interpretation is welcome.

### Current UI cues (no finalized brand yet)

- Primary color in the app: **blue**
- Onboarding accents used: **blue** (explore), **pink** (favorites), **green** (groups)
- No existing logo, icon system, or typography lock-in

### Motifs to explore

- Swipe gesture or card motion
- Map pin, compass, or path/journey
- Connection or “match” between people and places
- Travel without clichéd airplanes, passports, or globe stock imagery

### Practical constraints

- Must read clearly as an **app icon** from large (1024 px) down to small (64 px)
- Should work on **light backgrounds** (primary app theme is light)
- Symbol should stand alone without the wordmark where possible

## What to avoid

- **Dating-app clichés** — hearts-only or romance-coded branding (the swipe mechanic is borrowed, but the product is about *places*, not people)
- **Corporate travel aesthetic** — airline wings, hotel luxury, stiff serif wordmarks
- **Overly detailed icons** — fine lines and complex scenes disappear at small sizes
- **Illegible wordmarks** — “SwipeTrip” must remain readable at small scale in the wordmark variant
