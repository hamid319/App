Everything for your trip, in one place

No more ten apps, three spreadsheets and a group chat where everything gets lost.

  • 🃏Swipe togetherEveryone swipes through the places. What you all like goes on your wishlist.
  • 🗺️Days on a mapYour favourite places become short routes, day by day.
  • 💸Split costsScan receipts, share out the items, see who owes whom.
  • 📍Places nearbyOn the way, the app shows what is just around the corner.
  • 📔Travel journalPhotos and thoughts for each day, shared with everyone who was there.
  • 🛂StampsA stamp for every city. Check in on site and it shines in full colour.

Less planning stress. More memories together.

About us

Out We Go is made by Hamid and Ozan from Hamburg, Germany.

The idea came the way the best ideas do: in a group chat with 200 unread messages. One group of friends, one trip, and nobody could decide. Where to? When? Who books? And who still owes whom €23.50 for the pizza?

At some point we said: this has to be easier. So we built the app we wished we had. So you argue less and experience more. No ads, no selling data, just a lot of love for travelling.
