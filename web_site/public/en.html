Group trips, without the chaos in the chat.

Out We Go helps you plan a trip together: swipe places, sort the days on a map, split costs fairly and collect memories on the way.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

  • Swipe togetherEveryone swipes through the city's places. What you all like goes on your wishlist.
  • Days on a mapYour favourite places become short routes, day by day.
  • Split costsScan receipts, share out the items and see who owes whom.
  • Journal and stampsPhotos and notes for each day, and a stamp for every city you visit.

About Hozan Studio

Out We Go is made by Hamid Ebrahimi and Ozan Ermis from Hamburg, Germany. As Hozan Studio we build apps we like to use on our own trips: no ads, no selling data, with place data from open sources such as OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia.
