Support

Contact: hozanstudio@gmail.com

We usually reply within a few days. For a bug, it helps if you tell us your device and the steps that led to it.

How do I invite friends to a trip?

Open the trip and share the invite link. Anyone who already has the app can also tap + under "Trips" and enter the invite code.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email address. You will get a link to set a new password. Check your spam folder too.

A place is missing or wrong.

Our place data comes from OpenStreetMap, Wikidata and Overture Maps. Send us the place and what is wrong, and we will fix it in the next update of that city.

A review is inappropriate.

Report it in the app from the review itself. It is hidden right away and we check it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account. All steps, and what gets deleted, are on the Delete account page.
