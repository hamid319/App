Delete your Out We Go account

You can delete your account yourself at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Out We Go and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom, then the gear at the top right (Settings).
  3. Under "Account", tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete permanently and enter your password.

Without the app, by email

From your account's email address, write to hozanstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account". We delete your account within 30 days and confirm it by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, profile photo and PayPal.Me name
  • Your favourites, swipes, reviews and hidden-gem tips
  • Your packing lists and your journal photos
  • Trips where you are the only member, completely. You are removed from shared trips, and the longest-standing member becomes the organizer.
  • Your device tokens for notifications

What stays

Expenses, ideas and polls in shared trips stay, so the other members' plans and balances remain correct. They are shown without your name.

Google deletes your sign-in data from backups within 180 days. Crash reports, which are not linked to your account, are deleted by Google after 90 days. See the privacy policy for details.
